# Buildings

The **Builder's Workshop** in the Town screen lets you upgrade the permanent town buildings. Each building provides a passive bonus that scales with its tier. Upgrades require coins and Construction materials crafted at the {construction_link} skill.

Buildings start at Tier 0 (no bonus) and unlock higher tiers at higher Construction levels. Every Construction level also reduces upgrade coin and material costs by 0.5%, up to 49.5% at level 99.
