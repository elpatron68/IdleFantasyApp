# {icon} Construction

Craft furniture and structural items used to upgrade the town buildings in the **Builder's Workshop**, from the Inn and Guild Hall through late game projects like the Artisan's Workshop and Chronos Spire. See the [Buildings](Buildings) page for which items each building tier requires and what bonuses they provide.

Every Construction level also grants a **Builder's discount**: 0.5% off the coin and material costs of building upgrades per level, up to 49.5% at level 99.

Construction is session-based — each item takes 60 seconds to craft per unit.
